SECTION 1: PRE-FLOOD PREPAREDNESS
1.1 Households in low-lying or riverside areas should identify their
    nearest designated relief shelter before the monsoon season begins.
1.2 Keep an emergency kit ready containing drinking water, dry food, a
    torch, spare batteries, essential medicines, and copies of identity
    documents in a waterproof pouch.
1.3 Families with livestock should identify higher ground for relocating
    animals in advance of expected flooding.

SECTION 2: DURING ACTIVE FLOODING
2.1 Do not attempt to cross flowing flood water on foot or by vehicle,
    even if the depth appears shallow.
2.2 Move to higher floors or the designated community shelter immediately
    when local authorities issue an evacuation order.
2.3 Avoid contact with electrical installations and downed power lines in
    flooded areas.

SECTION 4: POST-FLOOD SAFETY
4.1 Do not consume water from sources that may have been contaminated by
    floodwater until it has been tested or treated.
4.2 Report damaged embankments or infrastructure to the local circle
    office before attempting any repairs.
